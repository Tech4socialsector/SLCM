Appendix III

Financial Aid and Scholarships

Students are admitted to NLSIU based on their academic performance in the admission process. The University has adopted an inclusion policy that ensures that students from diverse social, linguistic and economic backgrounds from across India benefit from a rigorous and transformative education. The admission process does not take into account the financial background of the applicant. Student fees for on-campus academic programmes cover only part of the actual cost of education, with the University subsidising the remaining cost. Further, the University is dedicated to ensuring that students admitted to an academic programme are provided with necessary support to continue their studies, irrespective of financial constraints. The Financial Aid Policy sets out the University's approach to delivering on this commitment.

Students whose gross family income is less than Rs. 8,00,000 per annum are eligible to apply for financial aid by the university. Before submitting their application for University's financial aid, candidates must apply under the PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme. NLSIU has been recognised by the Government of India under the PM-Vidyalaxmi Scheme as one of the leading higher educational institutions in the country. The PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme provides collateral-free and guarantor-free education loans to meritorious students in top institutions in India. There is no income limit to apply for this scheme, and loans up to Rs. 30 lakhs can be availed. Partial and full interest subvention is available for lower annual family incomes.

The application to PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme must be filled through the official PM Vidyalaxmi Portal: https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in before applying for financial aid with the University. For detailed guidelines & process, please visit PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme section of the universities scholarship page. A copy of the education loan application must be submitted along with the application for university financial aid. This is a pre-condition for applying for financial aid through the University. In the absence of this document, the financial aid process will be kept in abeyance.

Candidates who wish to apply for financial assistance under the University's Financial Aid Policy are required to submit their application for financial aid on or before {{ financial_aid_deadline }}, 11.59 p.m. The University will communicate its decision on the financial aid applications by 31 July 2026. Students who are provided financial aid shall have their fee reimbursed up to the extent of the aid provided. Application for financial aid does not exempt you from payment of the full fees before {{ full_fee_deadline }}.

Students who have been admitted to NLSIU may also be eligible for a number of scholarships sponsored by the central and state governments as well as private organisations. NLSIU has curated a list of some such scholarships for the benefit of candidates. Students may also visit the National Scholarship Portal for more information. For further assistance in this regard, please contact nlsiuscholarship@nls.ac.in.

A. Provisional offer, acceptance and portal

1. What does a "provisional" offer of admission mean?

It means that NLSIU is offering you admission subject to completion of all admission formalities. Your admission becomes final only after you accept the offer, pay the required fees within the deadlines, submit all required documents and forms, and successfully complete in-person verification of original documents at NLSIU.

2. How do I accept the provisional offer?

See Appendix I of the Provisional Offer of Admission.

3. What is the confirmation fee and when must it be paid?

You must pay Rs. {{ confirmation_fee_amt }} as the confirmation fee by {{ confirmation_deadline }}. Failure to pay the confirmation fee by the deadline will result in withdrawal of the admission offer.

4. Can I get the extension to pay the confirmation fees?

No. No extension will be granted for payment of confirmation fees.

5. Is the confirmation fee adjusted against the total fee payable?

Yes. After the confirmation fee is paid, NLSIU will generate a balance fee invoice. You will then be required to log back into the portal and pay the remaining amount by the full-fee deadline.

6. What if I do not want to accept the seat? What if I want to withdraw from the admission process?

Email nlsat.query@nls.ac.in from your registered email address. Your email should mention your full name, NLSAT application number, the programme offered, and a clear statement that you wish to withdraw your application. C. Financial aid, education loans and scholarships

18. Can I apply for financial aid?

Candidates whose gross family income is less than Rs. 8,00,000 per annum are eligible to apply for University financial aid. The definition of 'family income' is the average gross family income for the last three financial years or the gross family income for the latest financial year, whichever is greater. For the determination of family income, family is considered as members of a family residing together which includes self, parents, sibling, spouse / partner of the student.

19. Do I necessarily need to apply for PM Vidyalaxmi before applying for University financial aid?

Yes. Before submitting a University financial-aid application, candidates must apply through the official PM Vidyalaxmi portal at https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in and submit a copy or proof of the education-loan application along with the University financial-aid application. This is a pre-condition for the University financial-aid process.

20. Does applying for financial aid exempt me from paying the full fee by {{ full_fee_deadline }}?

No. For NLSAT admissions, applying for financial aid does not exempt you from paying the full fee by the deadline. Candidates must pay the full fee by {{ full_fee_deadline }}. If University financial aid is approved later, the fee will be reimbursed up to the extent of aid granted.

21. When will I know the outcome of my financial-aid application?

NLSIU will communicate decisions on financial-aid applications on or before 31 July 2026.

22. If I do not have my parents' income-tax returns, can I still apply for financial aid?

Yes. Where income-tax returns are not available, you may submit income certificates issued by government authorities, bank statements, and any other documents supporting the income earned by the family. The Financial Aid Committee may ask for further clarifications or documents.

23. Can I apply for other scholarships?

Yes. Admitted students may be eligible for scholarships sponsored by central and state governments and private organisations. NLSIU has curated a list of some scholarships, and candidates may also visit the National Scholarship Portal. For scholarship-related assistance, write to nlsiuscholarship@nls.ac.in.

24. Does financial aid cover the full fee?

Financial aid depends on the applicable policy and the assessment of the candidate's income and wealth information. The amount, if any, will be communicated by NLSIU. If granted after fee payment, reimbursement will be made up to the approved extent.

{# ════════════════════════════════════════ #} {# SECTION E — Category certificates #} {# ════════════════════════════════════════ #}

E. Category certificates and reservations

29. What is the validity requirement for an EWS certificate?

An EWS certificate must be valid at the time of admission and must not be older than one year, unless a different validity condition is specified in the certificate.

30. What is the validity requirement for an OBC-NCL certificate?

If the OBC-NCL certificate mentions a validity period, it must be within that validity period. If no validity period is mentioned, the certificate must not be older than one year. Candidates seeking OBC reservation must ensure that they meet the applicable category requirements.

31. Which authorities can issue valid OBC-NCL or EWS certificates?

OBC-NCL and EWS certificates should be issued by a competent authority such as a District Magistrate, Additional Magistrate, Collector, Deputy Commissioner, Additional Deputy Commissioner, Deputy Collector, First Class Stipendiary Magistrate, Sub-Divisional Magistrate, Taluka Magistrate, Executive Magistrate, Extra Assistant Commissioner not below the rank of First Class Stipendiary Magistrate, Chief Presidency Magistrate, Additional Chief Presidency Magistrate, Presidency Magistrate, Revenue Officer not below the rank of Tehsildar, or Sub-Divisional Officer of the area where the candidate or the candidate's family resides.

32. Can I use an Income and Caste Certificate instead of an EWS certificate?

No. A valid EWS certificate is required for EWS category eligibility. An Income and Caste Certificate is not sufficient for claiming reservation.

33. Can I rely on a State Government OBC certificate?

Candidates claiming OBC reservation must ensure that the category claimed falls within the central list or the Karnataka OBC list for the category claimed.

34. What is the validity for an SC/ST certificate?

SC/ST certificates generally do not have a validity period. However, the certificate must be genuine, issued by a competent authority, and valid for the category claimed.

35. What if my EWS, OBC-NCL, caste, disability or Karnataka document is under process?

You must produce the valid original certificate at the time of verification. A pending application or acknowledgement is not a substitute for the required certificate.

36. What should PWD candidates submit?

PWD candidates must submit the disability certificate in the format as per the The Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPwD) Act, 2016 (Form V or Form VI) signed and stamped by a competent authority or a valid Unique Disability ID(UDID) card.

37. What documents are required for the Karnataka Students category?

Study certificates from recognised and affiliated schools or educational institutions showing the required period of study in Karnataka are required.

38. Is a domicile certificate enough for the Karnataka Students category?

No. Domicile certificates are not accepted as a substitute for study certificates and other required documents.

39. Can study certificates issued in Kannada be submitted?

Yes. Study certificates issued in Kannada by recognised schools or educational institutions are acceptable.
