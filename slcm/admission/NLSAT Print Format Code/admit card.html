Instructions to Candidates
1. General Instructions
a.Candidates should check and review their admit cards carefully and make sure that their Name, Date of Birth, and other personal details mentioned in the admit card are as per the details filled by them in the application form. In case of any discrepancy, please write to nlsat.query@nls.ac.in.
b.Please carry a printed copy of this admit card to the test centre.
c.Candidates shall be required to follow all directions issued by the Centre Superintendent and the Institution representatives at their respective test centres.
2. Reporting to the Test Centre & Test Timings
a.The admissions test will commence at {{ formatted_start }}, and will be for a duration of {{ duration_mins }} minutes (two hours and thirty minutes). Candidates will be allowed to enter the premises of the test centre from {{ reporting_time }} and should be seated in the examination hall by {{ seated_time }}.
b.Candidates should carry a Government-issued photo ID card or a photo ID issued by a recognised educational institution. The ID card will be checked at the time of entry into the centre.
c.Candidates who arrive at the test centre beyond {{ late_time }} will not be permitted entry.
d.Once the test commences, Candidates will not be permitted to leave the examination hall until the test is completed, and all the Question Booklets and OMR response sheets have been collected by the invigilator/s.
3. Permitted Items
a.Candidates will only be permitted to carry the following inside the Test Centre:
i.Admit Card (In case the photograph on the Admit Card is not clear, candidates should carry a self-attested passport size photograph).
ii.Government Issued Photo ID or a photo ID issued by a recognised educational institution.
iii.Black or Blue Ballpoint pen.
iv.A transparent water bottle.
v.An analogue watch. Note: Smart Watches are not permitted.
4. Admissions Test Related Instructions
a.The {{ f_test or "Admissions Test" }} is for a maximum of {{ duration_mins }} minutes and will be conducted in English. The paper will consist of two parts – Part A and Part B.
b.An attendance sheet will be circulated during the admissions test. The requisite details should be filled in the attendance sheet.
c.Candidates will be provided with a Question Booklet and an Optical Marker Reader (OMR) response sheet to answer the admissions test.
d.Candidates should use black/blue ball point pen only to enter the admit card number, and other required details in the Question Booklet and the OMR response sheet. Details should be filled in the Question Booklet and the OMR response sheet only after commencement of the test at {{ formatted_start }}. The packet containing the Question Booklet and the OMR response sheet should not be opened prior to {{ formatted_start }}.
e.Part A will consist of 75 multiple choice questions (MCQs) which will be answered on the OMR response sheet. Candidates must read the instructions provided with the Question Booklet and OMR sheet before commencing the test.
f.Use a black/blue ball point pen only for shading the circles/answer bubbles in the OMR response sheet. Indicate only one answer by shading one among the options provided. The answer bubble/circle should be shaded completely without leaving any gap/space. If a bubble/circle is shaded incompletely, the question will be considered unanswered. If more than one bubble/circle is shaded in response to a question, that question too will be considered unanswered. Candidates should not make any stray marks anywhere on the OMR sheet.
g.Responses to questions in Part B of the paper shall be written in the space provided in the Question Booklet. Candidates should write the details required on the cover page of the Question Booklet.
h.Candidates are allowed to underline parts of the passage or questions. However, making any other marking or writing notes directly on the question paper is prohibited. Separate space is provided for making notes and for rough work.
i.No clarifications can be sought about the Question Booklet from anyone.
j.The OMR response sheet has a carbon copy. Candidates should return the original OMR response sheet (the first sheet), and their Question Booklet (with their answers to Part B of the paper) to the invigilator/s at the end of the admissions test. Candidates are permitted to carry the carbon copy (second sheet) of the OMR response sheet with them. Candidates shall not carry the Question Booklet out of the examination hall under any circumstances.
5. Marking Scheme
a.The {{ f_test or "Admissions Test" }} is for a total of 150 (One hundred and fifty) marks. Parts A and B are for 75 marks each. Every correct answer in Part A will be awarded one mark. Every wrong answer, and every unanswered question will lead to a deduction of 0.25 marks.
6. Documents to be retained by the Candidate after the test
a.The admit card, with the invigilator's signature should be retained by the candidate and produced at the Institution at the time of admission.
7. Malpractice
a.The use of any unfair means by a Candidate shall result in their disqualification, and cancellation of their test.
b.Impersonation is an offence, and the Candidate, apart from being disqualified, shall be liable to penal action under the law.
c.Possession of electronic devices, including mobile phones, headphones, earphones, smart watches, calculators etc., is strictly prohibited in the examination hall.
