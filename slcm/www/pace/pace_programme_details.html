Postgraduate Diploma in Arbitration Law

Academic Overview

The Postgraduate Diploma in Arbitration Law (PGDAL) at Lex Academia is meticulously designed for legal professionals, corporate advisors, and aspiring arbitrators. In an era where domestic and international commercial disputes increasingly rely on Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), this program provides the rigorous theoretical framework and practical skill sets required to excel in the arbitration arena.

Program Objectives

  • check_circle Mastery of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
  • check_circle Insights into International Commercial Arbitration.
  • check_circle Practical drafting of arbitration agreements and awards.
