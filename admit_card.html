Instructions to Candidates
1. General Instructions
a.Candidates should check and review their admit cards carefully and make sure that their Name, Date of Birth, and other personal details mentioned in the admit card are as per the details filled by them in the application form. In case of any discrepancy, please contact the Examination Cell immediately.
b.Please carry a printed copy of this admit card to the test centre.
2. Reporting to the Test Centre & Test Timings
a.Candidates will be allowed to enter the premises of the test centre 30 minutes before the examination start time and should be seated in the examination hall at least 15 minutes before commencement.
b.Candidates should carry a Government-issued photo ID card, preferably the one uploaded with the application form. The ID card will be checked at the time of entry into the centre.
c.Candidates who arrive at the test centre beyond the scheduled entry cut-off time will not be permitted entry.
d.Once the test commences, Candidates will not be permitted to leave the examination hall until the test is completed, and all the Question Booklets and OMR response sheets have been collected by the invigilator/s.
3. Pre-Test Instructions
a.Candidates shall be required to follow all directions issued by the Centre Coordinators and the Institution representatives at their respective test centres.
b.Candidates must maintain all protocols in place at their respective test centres.
4. Permitted Items
a.Candidates will only be permitted to carry the following inside the Test Centre:
i.Admit Card (In case the photograph on the Admit Card is not clear, candidates should carry a self-attested passport size photograph).
ii.Government Issued Photo ID (preferably the one uploaded with the application form).
iii.Black or Blue Ballpoint pen.
iv.A transparent water bottle.
v.A face mask (the Candidate may be asked to remove the face mask for ascertainment of identity).
vi.An analogue watch. Note: Smart Watches are not permitted.
5. Admissions Test Related Instructions
a.An attendance sheet will be circulated during the admissions test. The requisite details should be filled in the attendance sheet.
b.Candidates will be provided with a Question Booklet and/or answer sheets. Candidates should use black/blue ball point pen only to enter the admit card number and other required details.
c.Candidates must read the instructions provided with the Question Booklet before commencing the test.
d.Candidates should write the details required on the cover page of the Question Booklet.
e.No clarifications can be sought about the Question Booklet from anyone.
f.Candidates shall not carry the Question Booklet out of the examination hall under any circumstances.
6. Documents to be retained by the Candidate after the test
a.The admit card, with the invigilator's signature, should be retained by the candidate and produced at the Institution at the time of admission.
7. Malpractice
a.The use of any unfair means by a Candidate shall result in their disqualification and cancellation of their test.
b.Impersonation is an offence, and the Candidate, apart from being disqualified, shall be liable to penal action under the law.
c.Possession of electronic devices, including mobile phones, headphones, earphones, smart watches, calculators etc., is strictly prohibited in the examination hall.
